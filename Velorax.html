VELORAX Digital Store

A premium storefront for consumer electronics

Next.jsShopifyStripe

Overview

A premium e-commerce concept for VELORAX, designed to showcase high-ticket electronics with trust and conversion focus.

Problem

Electronics brands suffer low conversion due to cluttered UI and lack of premium feel.

Solution

Minimal dark theme, large imagery, sticky add-to-cart, comparison tables, trust badges, mobile-first checkout.

Features

360 product view, Smart cart, Reviews Q&A, Wishlist & Compare, SEO collection pages

Tech Stack

Figma, Next.js, Shopify Headless, Stripe, Tailwind
